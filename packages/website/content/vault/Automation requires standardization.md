When you create a software factory, you're automating workflows and in order to do that well with AI agents you need to clearly define your inputs and outputs.

Requires skills, skill hierarchies, agent roles, access to context, including research that's relevant to the work that needs to be done. You need to manage the environment that enables your agent automation.

I need to create a registry of skills that I just can use, keep those up to date, and we'll have to have agent roles access to specific context and skills so that they can execute on the jobs that they own.

Orchestration and concurrent or parallel work, you're going to need a way of managing the execution of the work they are doing. You're going to need a way of their progress and a way for them to manage up to you and let you know what they need to be successful. These are the same principles you use when managing a team of engineers.
